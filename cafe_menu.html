— Our Story

A Corner of Calm

Noir & Brew was born from a simple belief: good food and great coffee deserve a beautiful setting. Nestled in the heart of Dehradun, we source our beans from the misty hills of Coorg, our spices from old bazaars, and our recipes from generations of love. Every cup, every plate — a quiet ritual of pleasure.
